# sd-les-5
Makes: 2 quesadillas
Time: ~15 minutes

Ingredients
4 flour tortillas
4 to 6 strips bacon
150 g shredded cheddar cheese
half a small onion, finely diced (optional)
1 tbsp butter or oil
Pinch of black pepper
Salsa or sour cream, for serving
Method
Cook the bacon: Fry until crisp, then place on paper towel and chop into small pieces.
Prepare the filling: Mix the bacon with the shredded cheese. Add onion if you like.
Build the quesadillas: Put one tortilla in a skillet over medium heat. Sprinkle half with cheese and bacon, then fold the tortilla over.
Cook: Cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until golden and the cheese has melted. Repeat with the remaining tortillas and filling.
Slice & serve: Cut into triangles and serve hot with salsa or sour cream.

Tip: For extra crunch, don't overcrowd the tortilla and cook over medium rather than high heat.
